2021–2022 Industry ShopeePay

PDPA consent flow coordination

Coordinated Legal, Product and UX across the Thailand and Singapore teams to design a PDPA-compliant consent and opt-out flow before the first marketing email campaign went out. Covers consent capture, sender identity as an anti-impersonation control, and an opt-out path built to meet the Section 19 requirement that withdrawing consent be as easy as giving it. Delivered ahead of the PDPA enforcement date rather than in response to it.
